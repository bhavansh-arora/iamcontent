The discovery call

What happens when you book?

A short, free conversation. You don’t need to prepare anything or know exactly what you want.

  1. Pick a time

    Choose a slot that suits you on the calendar. Mornings, evenings and weekends are available.

  2. Talk for 15 minutes

    Share what’s on your mind. Ruchi listens and asks a few questions about where you’d like to go.

  3. Decide, no pressure

    If it feels right, you plan your sessions together. If not, you leave with a little more clarity.

Questions

Good questions to ask.

Still wondering? Write to ruchi@iamcontent.care

How is coaching different from therapy or counselling?
Therapy often looks back, helping you understand and heal from the past. Coaching looks forward, at where you want to go next and what is quietly holding you back. At I Am Content we hold both: the depth to understand where you’ve been, and the momentum to help you move.
What does a coaching program include?
Usually about three months of weekly 60-minute 1:1 online sessions, personalised reflective homework, breathwork and movement where helpful, psychological and coaching tools chosen for you, and SOS support for urgent moments.
Do you work online or in person?
Both. Sessions run online for clients anywhere in the world, and in person in Goa, India.
What approaches do you use?
Counselling, life coaching, yoga therapy and reflective practice. Rather than one lens, we use whichever approach, psychological, somatic or contemplative, serves what you are working through.
Can I solve my own problems, or do I need a coach?
You already hold the wisdom within. A coach doesn’t hand you answers; she helps you uncover the ones you already carry, and walks alongside you as you find your way back to yourself.
