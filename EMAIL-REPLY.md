Your six "coaching is right for you if…" points become a gentle self-check. When someone ticks two or three, the page tells them a 15-minute call is a good next step. Recognition turns into action.

Reflections now has a one-minute guided breathing circle, a sticky-note Wall and a café-style Watering Hole menu. Unsent Letters has tap-to-start prompts and a proper retrieval code. These are the moments people remember and share, and each one ends with a soft invitation to coaching.

Every page has proper search descriptions and structured data that helps Google understand who you are and what you offer, and images that are about 94% lighter (31 MB down to 1.9 MB), so it loads quickly even on mobile data.

Your forest green and the brand green from your logo, a hand-drawn fern that unfurls through the site (from your own illustration), your artwork pinned like journal pages, and quiet motion throughout. It works on phones and tablets and respects visitors who prefer reduced motion.
