The approach

Peeling the layers.

Lasting change comes from understanding our automatic response patterns and the vulnerabilities underneath them, not from surface fixes. Ruchi blends Western psychological counselling with Eastern reflective practices and yoga therapy to arrive at the root cause.

She works with identity shifts in motherhood, career transitions and friction in relationships, in person in Goa and online with clients around the world.

In Ruchi’s words

“My role is not to provide answers. It is to create the space where better questions can be asked.”
