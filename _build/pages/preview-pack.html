What happens next

Four calm steps.

Nothing changes on your live site until you’re happy.

  1. A 20-minute walkthrough

    We click through it together and you tell us what feels like you, and what doesn’t.

  2. Your edits

    Wording, pictures, programs. It’s your story, so you have the final say on every line.

  3. Connect the pieces

    Forms to your inbox, the Wall and letters to your back end, and the note field in your Calendly.

  4. Go live

    On iamcontent.care, keeping your existing page addresses so nothing breaks in Google.

An honest note

What’s real, and what’s waiting.

  • Every program, quote, rating, picture and line of your copy comes from your current site.
  • The forms aren’t connected yet, so nothing is sent anywhere. Letters, Wall notes and the waitlist work in the browser for now.
  • The six notes on the Wall are examples, clearly labelled. We’d replace them with real, moderated notes.
  • The Loop Check statements and the rewritten “shoulds” are drafted from your words, for you to approve or change.
