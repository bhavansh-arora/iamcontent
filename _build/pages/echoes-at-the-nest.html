Inside the book

Three threads, woven together.

Not static portraits of parenting, but the quiet shifts of a generational journey.

{{ICON:users}}

The evolving bond between mothers and children

The space between grown daughters and their mothers: unspoken understandings, renegotiated boundaries, and the emotional inheritance passed quietly down the line. How we learn to see our parents as full human beings.

{{ICON:compass}}

Identity, self-discovery and transition

Before we can give and nurture, we need to discover who we are. Any change in a relationship is a life transition. It asks us to leave something behind and take on something new.

{{ICON:mail}}

Unsent letters & quiet reflections

So much is left unsaid, and so much is said more to oneself than to another. Letters written to the heart rather than a postbox: forgiveness, late-night realisations, everyday moments.

Who it’s for

For mothers, and the children who grow up.

  • Mothers making sense of motherhood in all its seasons
  • Adult children wanting to understand their mothers
  • Anyone holding words they never got to say
  • A thoughtful gift for a mother, daughter or friend
