Life coaching philosophy

Everyone has a story worth telling.

A story shaped by life’s ordinary and extraordinary moments. Drama, comedy, action, joy and grief, courage and fear, victory and loss.

The wisdom within

Every person carries the wisdom to navigate life and write their own story. But sometimes that wisdom gets quieter, covered by expectations of who we should be, how we should love, how life should unfold.

Stories that no longer fit

who I should bewho I am

how I should lovehow I love

how life should unfoldhow my life unfolds

and slowly, I am content.

A wet umbrella

Some stories no longer fit.

We collect beliefs, rules and habits that once served us. Like a wet umbrella left open inside the house: useful while the rain was falling, but now it just gets in the way. We tiptoe around it, adjusting our lives to something we no longer need.

This is a space to pause, reconnect with wonder, release some fears and let go of the labels that are no longer in fashion for us. To not be recognised by the roles we play. To be simply seen.

How the work is made

Four practices, one space.

Not to solve people’s problems, but to help them discover the answers that already exist within them.
